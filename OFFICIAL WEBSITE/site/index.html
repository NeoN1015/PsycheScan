Reading the indicators

Change from baseline

Heart rate and GSR are shown as a percentage above or below the patient's resting baseline. Temperature is shown as the drop (or rise) in °C from the baseline. These are plain measurements, not stress levels.

LSTM

When a trained model is installed, it reads the last few seconds of vital signs and reports its label with a confidence percentage. It only runs when at least 80% of that window has valid readings.

Face and fatigue

Expression probabilities and the fatigue, eyebag and wrinkle cues come from the camera. Lighting and camera angle affect them.

Decision-support tool only. Not a diagnosis. Does not replace clinical assessment.
